WHO WE ARE

About CIDH-BU

Community Innovation and Development Hub Burundi (CIDH-BU) is a company committed to promoting sustainable community development in Burundi.

Building stronger communities through innovation

CIDH-BU works closely with youth, women, and vulnerable populations to strengthen their capacities, improve livelihoods, and enhance their active participation in local economic development.

Our approach integrates practical skills training, entrepreneurship support, innovation, and community-based initiatives to generate lasting social and economic impact.

We apply a hands-on "learning-by-doing" approach that transforms ideas into practical solutions and sustainable enterprises.

Our Purpose

To enhance the well-being of youth, women, and vulnerable populations by promoting innovation, sustainable livelihoods, and entrepreneurship, while strengthening community resilience, self-reliance, and access to psychosocial support.

◈

Our Vision

To become a leading community innovation and development hub in Burundi, promoting entrepreneurship, inclusive growth, resilience, and sustainable livelihoods.

◎

Our Mission

To empower youth, women, and vulnerable communities through innovation, quality training, entrepreneurship, psychosocial support, and sustainable development initiatives that improve livelihoods, resilience, and social well-being.

WHAT WE AIM TO ACHIEVE

Our Objectives

Our strategic objectives are designed to promote sustainable development, empower communities, improve livelihoods, and strengthen resilience.

01

Empower Communities

To empower youth, women, and vulnerable populations through innovative, skills-based training, entrepreneurship development, and integrated social support services.

02

Promote Innovation

To promote practical innovation and community-based enterprises by transforming ideas into market-oriented products and services, including agribusiness, digital solutions, and local service initiatives.

03

Improve Livelihoods

To improve livelihoods through sustainable income-generating activities by equipping beneficiaries with entrepreneurial skills, financial literacy, and access to economic opportunities.

04

Strengthen Resilience

To strengthen community resilience and self-reliance by enhancing adaptive capacities, local problem-solving skills, and sustainable resource utilisation.

05

Promote Inclusive Development

To promote inclusive and environmentally responsible development by ensuring equal participation while integrating eco-friendly and climate-smart practices.

06

Build Strategic Partnerships

To build strategic partnerships for long-term community impact by collaborating with government institutions, NGOs, private-sector actors, and development partners.

07

Provide Psychosocial Support

To provide counselling services and psychosocial support aimed at improving mental well-being, social integration, and overall quality of life.
